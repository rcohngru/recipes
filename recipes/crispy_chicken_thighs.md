# Crispy Chicken Thighs
[Basics](../lists/basics.md), [Meats](../lists/meats.md)

### Timing
- Total Time: ~30 minutes
- Active Cooking Time: ~10 minutes

### Ingredients
- Skin-on Bone-in Chicken Thighs
- Salt

### Directions
1. Season skin-side of thighs with salt. Place skin-side down in a cold stainless steel or cast iron pan.
2. Place pan over medium-high heat. Season the non-skin-side of the thighs with salt. DO NOT TOUCH the thighs for at least 20 minutes.
3. After 20+ minutes, the skin of the chicken should be crisping up from the rendered fat of the thighs, allowing you to gently pull the thighs off the pan to flip them.
4. Flip the thighs and finish cooking, roughly 7-10 minutes.

### Notes
By placing the thighs in a cold pan, the process of heating the pan will allow the fat from the thighs to render better, creating an oil in which the thighs fry in.

Before putting the thighs on the stove, I like to stack another heavy pot or pan on top of them to weigh them down, so that there is more surface contact with the pan. You can put parchment paper in between to avoid dirtying another dish.

This is a great base for a weeknight meal. You can finish it with some sauces like [Hariss](harissa.md) and [Lemon Tahini Dressing](lemon_tahini_dressing.md) and some sauteed vegetables.
