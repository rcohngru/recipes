# Harissa
[Sauces](../lists/dressings_marinades_sauces_salsas.md)

### Timing
- Total Time: ~60 minutes
- Active Cooking Time: ~60 minutes

### Ingredients
- Red bell pepper
- 6-8 Dried chile de arbol
- 3 Dried california chile
- 1 Dried Guajillo chile
- 1tsp cumin seeds
- 1tsp coriander seeds
- 0.5tsp caraway seeds
- 4 cloves garlic
- Juice of half a lemon
- 1tsp salt
- 1 tbsp tomato paste

### Directions
1. Char bell pepper over gas stove or broil in oven until fully blackended on all sides, rotating periodically. Place in heat-proof bowl to steam for 15+ minutes. Remove charred skin, stem, seeds, and pith, set aside.
2. Deseed/destem dried chilies. Place in heat-proof bowl and cover with boiling water to rehydrate for 15+ minutes. Drain and set aside.
3. Toast seeds in pan over medium heat until fragrant, 3-5 minutes. Grind into powder using grinder or mortart & pestle.
4. Combine all ingredients in food processor and blend until smooth.
5. Transfer sauce to a clean jar and store in fridge. Cover with olive oil after each use to last longer.

### Notes
Really good as a topping on top of [Crispy Chicken Thighs](crispy_chicken_thighs.md) alongside [Lemon Tahini Dressing](lemon_tahini_sauce.md)
