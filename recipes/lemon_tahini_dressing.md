# Lemon Tahini Dressing
[Dressings](../lists/dressings_marinades_sauces_salsas.md)

### Timing
- Total Time: ~5 minutes
- Active Cooking Time: ~5 minutes

### Ingredients
- Tahini
- Lemon
- Garlic
- Salt
- Water

### Directions
1. Mince garlic into a fine paste.
2. Combine tahini, lemon juice, and garlic in bowl. Whisk until thick.
3. Slowly add water into mixture, whisking continuously, until desired viscosity is reached.
4. Season to taste with salt.

### Notes
Didn't include measurements because this is so variable, but generally use like 1/4C of tahini, 1/2-1 lemon, 1 clove of garlic.
