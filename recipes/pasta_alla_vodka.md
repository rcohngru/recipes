# Pasta Alla Vodka
[Basics](../lists/basics.md), [Pasta](../lists/pasta.md)

### Timing
- Total Time: ~30 minutes
- Active Cooking Time: ~15 minutes

### Ingredients
- 1 Yellow onion
- 4 Garlic cloves
- Parmigiano reggiano
- 1 Tube tomato paste, double-concentrated preferred
- 0.5tsp Crushed red pepper flakes (optional)
- 0.25C Vodka
- 0.75C Heavy cream
- 1 lb Pasta
- Olive oil
- Kosher salt

### Directions
1. Bring large pot of salted water to boil. Cook pasta until al dente, reserve ~1C of pasta water.
2. Peel & finely chop onion.
3. Smash & chop garlic.
4. Add oil to large pot/pan over medium heat. Add onion and garlic and cook until onion is beginning to brown, 5-7 minutes. Stir continuously.
5. Add entire tube of tomato paste and crushed red pepper flakes to pan. Stir until the paste evenly coats all of the onion/garlic mixture. Continue to cook until the paste takes on a deep red color, 5-7 minutes. Stir frequently.
6. Add vodka to deglaze pan, stir to incorporate, reduce heat to low.
7. Combine heavy cream & 0.25C of pasta water to warm the cream up, then add to pan. Mix to combine everything.
8. Add as much grated parmigiano reggiano as your heart desires, then add some more. Stir to combine. Add more pasta water in if needed, to result in a smooth sauce.
9. Add cooked pasta to pan, mix to combine.

### Notes
To add more protein, you can cook up some ground beef and add into the mixture as well.
