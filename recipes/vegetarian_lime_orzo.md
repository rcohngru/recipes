# Vegetarian Lime Orzo
[Pasta](../lists/pasta.md)

### Timing
- Total Time: ~55 minutes
- Active Cooking Time: ~35 minutes

### Ingredients
- olive oil
- 2-4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 medium zucchini, peeled & shredded
- 2-3 carrots, peeled & shredded
- 3-4 green onions, chopped
- 2 C orzo
- 1 (16oz) can stewed tomatoes, undrained
- 1 (16oz) can vegetable broth
- 1 tsp italian seasoning
- 1 tsp dried basil
- zest & juice of 1 lime
- parmigiano reggiano
- salt


### Directions
1. Prep vegetables.
2. Heat oil in large skillet or dutch oven. Stir in garlic & orzo, cook and stir pasta until golden, 5 minutes.
3. Add zucchini and carrots. Stir and cook until vegetables soften, 2 minutes.
4. Stir in tomatoes, broth, seasoning. Reduce heat to medium, cover, and simmer until almost all liquid is absorbed, 10 minutes.
5. Stir in green onions, lime zest, lime juice. Add as much grated parmigiano reggiano as you desire.
